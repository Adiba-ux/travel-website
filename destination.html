Popular Destinations

Bangladesh has many beautiful destinations for travellers. Cox's Bazar is famous for its long natural sea beach, while Sylhet is known for its green hills, tea gardens and natural scenery.

The Sundarbans is another important destination in Bangladesh. It is a large mangrove forest with unique wildlife and beautiful natural surroundings. These destinations offer different types of experiences for visitors.

Cox's Bazar

Cox's Bazar is known for its long sandy beach and beautiful views of the Bay of Bengal.

Sylhet

Sylhet is famous for its tea gardens, green hills and peaceful natural environment.

The Sundarbans

The Sundarbans is a famous mangrove forest with rich biodiversity and natural beauty.
